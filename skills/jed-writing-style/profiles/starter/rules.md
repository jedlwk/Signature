# Rules

A blank profile's rules. They hold for anyone. Add your own below them as you find them.

1. Point first. Answer in the first sentence.
2. Use only the facts you were given. Never invent a number, quote, name, example or anecdote. If
   one is missing, use a placeholder like [number] or ask.
3. When rewriting the writer's own text, keep their examples, quotes, numbers and names.
4. No chatbot habits: no counted preambles, throat-clearing, closing summaries, slogans or hollow
   praise.
5. End on the fact, the next step or a real question.

## Your rules

Add one line for each thing you keep changing in drafts. `/signature:learn` can suggest them from your
edits.
