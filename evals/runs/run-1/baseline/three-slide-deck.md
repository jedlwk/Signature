# 5-minute talk: three slides

## Slide 1: The problem (about 1.5 min)
- Title: 10,000 documents a month, reviewed by hand
- One big number: 10,000 documents / month
- Three short points: it's slow, people read differently from each other, and good reviewers spend their time on routine files
- Visual: a pile of documents against a calendar

## Slide 2: What we built (about 1.5 min)
- Title: A tool that reads first, people decide
- Simple flow: document in -> tool extracts and flags -> reviewer checks flagged items -> decision
- Three short points: what it reads, what it flags, where a person stays in charge
- One result to show: review time per document, before and after

## Slide 3: Live demo (about 2 min)
- Title: Let's run one
- Steps on screen: upload a document, watch it get read, open a flagged item, approve or correct it
- Backup: a screenshot of the finished result in case the connection fails
- Close with one question to the audience: which of your queues looks like this?
