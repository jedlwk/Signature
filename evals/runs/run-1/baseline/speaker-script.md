**Slide 1: The problem (about 90 seconds)**

Let me start with a number. Ten thousand. That's how many documents our team reviews every month. By hand.

Think about what that means. Someone opens a document, reads it, checks it against what we need, makes a note, and moves on to the next one. Then they do it again. And again. Ten thousand times.

It's slow, of course. But that's not the part that worries me most.

The part that worries me is that two people can read the same document and come to different conclusions. Especially at four in the afternoon, on the three hundredth file of the week.

And the people doing this are not junior. They are the ones who know the work best. Most of their day goes to files that turn out to be routine, and the tricky ones wait in the same queue as everything else.

So the question I want us to think about is simple. What would it look like if our best reviewers only spent their time on the documents that actually need them?

That's what we set out to fix. Let me show you what we built.
