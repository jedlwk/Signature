# Slide 1: The problem

**Manual review: 10,000 documents a month**

Teams read every page. It takes weeks. The risk is that important details get missed when volume gets this high.

# Slide 2: What we built

**Screening in minutes, not weeks**

The tool flags documents that need attention. A human reads the summary, not the full text. Quality stays high, time drops from weeks to days.
