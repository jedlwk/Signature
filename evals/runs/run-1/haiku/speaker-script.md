[Problem slide]

Think about what happens when your team gets ten thousand pages a month to review. I'm not talking about ten or a hundred. I mean ten thousand. Every single one gets read by a human. They go through the cover, the summary, the details. They flag what matters. It takes weeks. Some get missed because the volume is just too high. One team I worked with had a backlog for months because they couldn't hire fast enough to keep up.

You can't speed up humans. You can't ask someone to read faster and catch more. The only way forward is to stop asking them to read everything.

So what if the tool read first? It flags the ones that need attention. Your team reviews the summary instead of the full document. That's what we built.
