Who actually checks the AI's work?

Earlier this week, I had the privilege of spending the morning with over 30 HR leaders. We built a small screening tool for CVs, no code required. Everyone got one working in the first hour.

Then something interesting happened. The room asked the same question at once: who checks what the AI flagged? Nobody had an answer.

The tool worked. The screens were clean. But the moment you ask someone to act on an AI result, you need a human in the loop. It sounds obvious once you say it out loud. I think most teams figure it out the hard way.

That's what stayed with me: it's not the AI that worries people. It's the gap between what the system decides and who takes responsibility for the call.
