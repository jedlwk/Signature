How many documents does your team read in a month?

For us, the answer is ten thousand. Every one of them is read by a person.

Think about what that means. Someone opens a document. They find the part that matters. They check it, then move to the next one. Then they do it again. Hour after hour, day after day.

That's a lot of hours. It's also slow, careful work. It's hard to keep up for a whole day, let alone a whole month. And tired people miss things. It's not about how good they are. It's what ten thousand documents a month does to anyone's attention.

So what do teams do? They add more people, or they accept the delays. Neither one scales. Neither one fixes the real problem. Ten thousand becomes twelve thousand, and the pile only grows.

Here's what we wanted to know. It's a simple question. What if the first read didn't have to be done by a person?
