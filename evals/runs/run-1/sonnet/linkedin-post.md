Who checks the AI's work?

Last week, I ran a workshop for 30 HR leaders. None of them write code. By the end, each had built a small CV screening tool.

It was really fun to watch. But the question that took over the room wasn't how to build it. It was who checks what the tool decides.

That is the right question. A screening tool that nobody reviews is a risk. A person who reads its shortlist and can overrule it is a safeguard. So the tool matters less than the habit around it: a named person, a clear look at what the tool got wrong, and a say in the final call.

I came away thinking the build is the easy part. Deciding who signs off is the work HR leaders are best placed to do.
