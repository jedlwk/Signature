If AI screens the CVs, who checks the AI?

Last week I ran a workshop for 30 HR leaders. None of them wrote a line of code. By the end, they had built a small tool that screens CVs.

Once the tool was working, the room kept coming back to one question. Who checks its work?

It's a fair question. A tool that sorts CVs is making a call about real people. Someone has to look at what it passed over, and someone has to own the final decision.

What stayed with me is that nobody asked whether AI belongs in hiring. They asked how to stay accountable once it is there. That's the right place to start!!!

For me, that's now the first thing to settle before any AI tool goes near a hiring decision.
