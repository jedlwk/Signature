# AI audit findings

## P0 (always fix)

**"pivotal hub"** - Inflated significance. Say what it actually does or is. Example: "a central tool", "the hub of workflow", or just drop the metaphor.

## P1 (fix unless deliberate)

**"robust"** - Banned LLM word. Say "strong", "reliable", or the specific attribute: "handles 10,000 tasks" is more honest than "robust".

**"serves as"** - Copula avoidance. Just say "is": "Moreover, it is a hub for..."

**"fast-paced landscape"** - LLM vocabulary. "Landscape" is a dead metaphor here. Say what's actually fast: "In fast-moving markets" or skip the preamble.

**"seamlessly empowers teams to unlock value"** - Multiple LLM tells. "Seamlessly" is filler, "empowers" and "unlock" are AI vocabulary. Rewrite plainly: "helps teams get results" or name what you actually do.

**"Moreover"** - Connector word that reads as generated. Use "Also" or start a fresh sentence.

## What to fix first

Start with P0 and the banned words. Then rebuild the sentence plainly: what does your platform actually do? Skip the metaphors and say it straight.
