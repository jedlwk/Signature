Where does AI actually belong in HR?

Earlier this week I spent the morning with over 30 HR leaders. We built a small CV screening tool together. No code, no templates, just a spreadsheet and a few prompts.

What struck me was the question everyone asked: who checks the AI's work? Not whether to use AI. Not how to prompt it. Who sits between the model and a hiring decision?

It's the real question in every room I'm in now. Not "can we automate this" but "who's responsible when we do?"
