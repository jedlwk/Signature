Who checks the AI's work?

That was the big question in the room last week. I ran a workshop for 30 HR leaders, and they built a small CV screening tool with no code!!

I was really glad that was the question. A CV screen is a decision about a real person. So before anyone talks about speed, someone has to own the result. Who reviews the shortlist? Who notices when it gets one wrong?

HR leaders are well placed to answer that, because judgement calls about people are already their job.

For me, that is the real work of bringing AI into a team. The tool is quick to show. Deciding who checks it is where I want every team to start.
