How long does it take to read 10,000 documents?

That's the number we deal with every month. Ten thousand documents, and every one of them is reviewed by hand.

Picture what that means. A person opens a document and reads it. Then they decide what happens next. Then they open the next one, and the one after that.

[a short story from the project]

Manual review is slow, and it's tiring work. The hard part is keeping the same care on the last document of the month as on the first. When the pile is this big, people get through it as fast as they can.

It also doesn't shrink. A new month starts, and there's another 10,000 waiting for us.

Nobody here is doing anything wrong. People work hard on this, and the care they bring is real. The process just wasn't built for this volume. It was built when the pile was smaller, and the pile kept growing.

So the problem isn't that people can't do the review. They can. The problem is that we're asking them to do all of it, by hand, every single month.

Our question was simple. Could a person spend their time on the documents that need them? That's what we set out to fix. So here's what we built.
